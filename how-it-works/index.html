How it works

Clear steps from your current profile to a better-managed Google presence.

You do not need to know SEO jargon. We explain what we found, what we recommend, what we changed and what comes next.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the business, not a checklist.

We first need the basic context: what you sell, where you serve, which services matter and what customers should understand when they find you.

  • Business name and website
  • Location or service area
  • Main services or products
  • Current Google Business Profile access when work begins
Optional visual area — add your onboarding illustration here later.
02 / AUDIT

Inspect the profile carefully.

We review the important profile fields, categories, services, hours, attributes, photos, Q&A, reviews and recent activity.

  • Find incomplete or outdated information
  • Check category and service structure
  • Review customer-facing content
Optional profile-audit visual can go here later.
03 / RESEARCH

Look at the local market.

For packages that include deeper research, we compare relevant competitors and local search themes before deciding what deserves priority.

  • Duo competitor analysis
  • Relevant service and location themes
  • Gaps worth addressing
04 / OPTIMIZE

Make the agreed improvements.

We organize the profile and make the changes included in your package. The work is practical: fewer unnecessary edits, more attention to the details that matter.

  • Profile fields and categories
  • Services and description
  • Photos, Q&A and content where included
Optional before/after profile visual can go here later.
05 / MANAGE

Keep the profile maintained.

Monthly management turns the one-time setup into an ongoing routine: monitor, respond, update and report without making you chase every small task.

  • Review monitoring and responses
  • Google Posts and profile updates
  • Monthly performance reporting
