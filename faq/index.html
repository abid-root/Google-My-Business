01

Getting started

Choose the right package and know what happens next.

Which package should I choose?+

Profile Optimization ($149) is for a focused one-time cleanup and improvement. Optimization Plus ($249) adds deeper local research and refinement. Full Google Management ($349/month) is for businesses that want ongoing profile care, review management and regular updates.

What information do you need from me?+

Usually your business name, website, Google Business Profile link, location or service area, current services, preferred contact details and any business information you want reflected on Google. We only ask for information relevant to the agreed work.

How do we communicate during the project?+

The service is designed around written communication. You can use the Fiverr order, email or WhatsApp to share information, questions and approvals. No audio or video meeting is required to begin.

How long does each package take?+

Profile Optimization is normally delivered in 3–5 business days. Optimization Plus is normally 10–14 business days because it includes deeper research. Timing can vary when access, verification or client-provided information takes longer.

02

Work & delivery

Know what the work covers and what you receive.

What areas of my profile can you improve?+

Depending on the package, we can work on business information, primary and additional categories, services, description, hours, service areas, attributes, photos, Google Posts, Q&A, review responses and other relevant profile details.

Does the work include local competitor research?+

Yes, where included in the package. Optimization Plus and Full Google Management use research to help guide profile decisions. The depth of research depends on the scope and number of locations.

What will I receive after a one-time project?+

You receive the agreed profile work plus a clear summary of the main improvements and practical next steps. The exact deliverables follow the package description and your order scope.

Can I review or approve information before it is changed?+

Yes. When a detail depends on your business knowledge or approval, we can ask you to confirm it first. You remain the owner and decision-maker for your business information.

Can you help with reviews?+

Full Google Management includes review monitoring and response management within the agreed scope. We use genuine business information and do not create, buy or fabricate customer reviews.

03

Access & account security

Keep your Google account and business ownership in your hands.

Do you need my Google password?+

No. Google provides owner and manager roles so businesses can grant access without sharing a Google password. FlowSync uses the appropriate access level when management access is needed.

How do I keep my account access safe?+

Keep yourself as the Business Profile owner, use a manager invitation for third-party help, keep 2-Step Verification enabled on your Google account, and review profile access periodically. Google also recommends limiting access to people who need it.

Do I need to send a Google verification code, OTP or recovery code?+

No. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes or personal Google account security information through Fiverr, email or WhatsApp. If Google asks you to complete a verification step, you should complete it directly through Google.

What about a government ID or business document?+

A Google verification process may sometimes ask the business owner for documentation. FlowSync does not need your personal government ID for normal profile optimization. If Google requests identity or business verification, handle that request directly through Google's own verification flow whenever possible.

Do I keep ownership after working with FlowSync?+

Yes. Your business should remain under your ownership. Google states that businesses should retain control of their profile and that third-party managers should use the appropriate access role.

04

Monthly management

Understand what ongoing support looks like each month.

What is included in Full Google Management?+

The $349/month service includes initial optimization plus ongoing review monitoring and responses, up to 4 Google Posts per month, profile/service/photo updates, Q&A and information monitoring, ongoing optimization and a monthly report for one business location.

How will I know what was worked on?+

Monthly clients receive a clear report covering the main work completed and useful next steps. The goal is to make the service easy to understand without requiring a meeting.

Can the monthly service adapt as my business changes?+

Yes. Ongoing management is designed to keep important profile information current as services, hours, photos, customer questions and business priorities change.

Can you manage more than one location?+

Yes. Standard pricing covers one business location. Multiple locations are scoped separately so the workload, access and reporting stay clear.

How do I change or end monthly management?+

You can ask about cancellation or changes through the same channel used for your order. Fiverr orders also follow Fiverr's applicable payment and cancellation terms.

Will you guarantee a specific Google ranking?+

The service focuses on accurate information, relevant optimization, useful activity and ongoing maintenance. Google does not provide third parties with a special method to guarantee a particular Search or Maps position.
