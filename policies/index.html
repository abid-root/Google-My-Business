Policies & client information

Clear service terms, privacy and account-safety practices.

This page explains how FlowSync works with client information, Google Business Profile access, project scope, communication and ongoing services.

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Plain-language service information, not legal advice

1. Service terms

FlowSync provides Google Business Profile optimization and management as an independent third-party service provider. Each order is limited to the package, business location and deliverables agreed with the client.

Clear scope before work starts

The package price covers the work described on the Pricing page and in the order. If a request falls outside that scope, we will explain the additional work before proceeding.

Client information and accuracy

Clients remain responsible for confirming business facts such as the legal or real-world business name, address or service area, hours, services, contact details and other information that should appear on Google. We use the information you provide and ask for clarification when something is unclear.

Results and rankings

Google Search and Maps visibility depends on many factors outside FlowSync's control. We do not promise a specific ranking, number of reviews, calls, leads, visits or revenue.

2. Google Business Profile ownership & access

You keep ownership of your Business Profile. When access is needed for management, the preferred setup is for the business owner to invite FlowSync as a manager using Google's access controls. Google specifically advises businesses to retain ownership and avoid sharing passwords with third parties.

Safe access ruleNever send your Google password, 2-Step Verification code, recovery code or other Google account security credentials to FlowSync. A manager invitation is enough for normal third-party profile work.

Access changes

You can review who has access to your Business Profile and remove third-party access when management ends. FlowSync does not claim ownership of a client's profile and will not intentionally block the owner from accessing or controlling the profile.

3. Account & information security

We follow a minimum-access approach: only information and profile access needed for the agreed work should be shared. Google recommends limiting profile access to people who need it and using manager access for third-party help.

What we do not need

  • Google account passwords
  • One-time passwords or verification codes
  • Google recovery codes
  • Personal banking passwords or card security codes
  • Personal government ID for normal profile optimization

Verification documents

If Google requests identity or business verification, the safest route is for the business owner to complete that process directly through Google. Do not send sensitive identity documents to FlowSync unless a specific, lawful service requirement has been agreed in advance.

Business information

Project information such as business descriptions, service lists, photos, website details and profile links is used to prepare and deliver the requested service and communicate about the project. We do not sell client contact information.

4. Privacy & data handling

Information we may receive

You may choose to provide your name, business name, email address, website, Google Business Profile link, business details, service information, photos, written instructions and project-related messages.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare profile recommendations, complete ordered work, communicate with you and provide reports or follow-up support related to the service.

Website forms

The profile-check form on this static website opens an email draft addressed to FlowSync. It is not a database-backed form. Your email provider and the services you use to send the message may process the information according to their own policies.

Third-party platforms

Orders may be placed through Fiverr, and communication may take place through email or WhatsApp when you choose those channels. Those services have their own privacy and security practices. Google Business Profile information is also handled within Google's systems when you authorize profile management.

Retention

We aim to keep only information that is reasonably needed for active work, order records, communication and follow-up. If you want a specific project detail removed from FlowSync's working records, contact us and we will review the request, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate business, payment or dispute purposes.

5. Communication & approvals

Normal project communication is written through the Fiverr order, email or WhatsApp. You can send approvals, corrections and business information in writing. No audio or video meeting is required unless both sides separately agree that one would be useful.

Client approvals

When a proposed change depends on information only the business owner can confirm, we may ask for written approval before applying it. This helps keep the profile accurate and keeps the owner in control.

6. Reviews & policy-compliant work

FlowSync can help monitor and respond to reviews within the selected service. We do not create, purchase or fabricate reviews, use misleading business information, impersonate Google or promise a special ranking method. Work is intended to follow Google Business Profile policies.

7. Payment, cancellation & refunds

Payment and refund terms depend on the platform and order used to purchase the service. For Fiverr orders, Fiverr's applicable terms and the specific order agreement apply. If you have a billing or cancellation question, ask before placing the order so the scope is clear.

8. Policy updates

These policies may be updated when the service, website, payment process or applicable requirements change. The latest version on this page applies to future work unless a written order agreement states otherwise.
